Hermes Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Hermes is a personal, single-user application. It accesses Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Contacts) only for the account owner who authorized it, and only to perform tasks the owner requests.

  • Data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties.
  • Data is not used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are stored privately on the owner's own server.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
